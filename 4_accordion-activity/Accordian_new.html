Responsible Use of AI in Learning

Use AI to support your learning

Use AI to brainstorm ideas and explore explanations. Review and refine its suggestions, and use your own judgement to decide what supports your learning goal.

Verify AI output before using it

Check facts and claims against reliable source material. Look for errors, missing context, bias, or unsupported claims. Correct or remove information that you cannot verify.

Protect information and take responsibility

Avoid entering confidential workplace information or personal data into unapproved AI tools. Follow your organisation’s guidance and take responsibility for the accuracy and appropriateness of the content you share.
